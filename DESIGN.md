# Prakriti Peacock Feather (मयूर पिच्छ) Design System

## Sacred Concept
The sacred peacock feather (*Mor Pankh* / मयूर पिच्छ), gracefully adorning Shri Krishna's crown, serves as the divine optic inspiration for Prakriti's two circadian modes:

### 1. Raadha Mode (Light Mode · राधा भाव)
- **Concept**: Morning daylight, pure solar radiation, reverent nourishment of soil and plants.
- **Background**: Luminous Quill White (`#FAFCF8`)
- **Cards & Surfaces**: Soft Feather Silk (`#FFFFFF`, `#F6FAF7`, `#EDF5F1`)
- **Primary Accent (Giving Arc & Highlights)**: Mayur Kanth Teal (`#097770`)
- **Secondary Accent (Solar / Energy)**: Chandrika Molten Gold (`#C58F1B` / `#D4AF37`)
- **Flora & Plumes**: Emerald Plumes (`#1A5F44`)
- **Borders**: Quill Shaft Border (`#D2E3DB`)
- **Typography**: Sacred Peacock Ink (`#0C1F1B`) & Feather Slate (`#3E564F`)

### 2. Krishna Mode (Dark Mode · कृष्ण रूप / Krisha Mode)
- **Concept**: Iridescent midnight celestial twilight, cooling star silence, sacred reflection.
- **Background**: Celestial Peacock Midnight (`#07131B`)
- **Inner Screens**: Midnight Blue Shadow (`#091A26`)
- **Cards & Surfaces**: Iridescent Midnight Teal (`#0D212E`, `#102B3C`)
- **Primary Accent (Lightness Arc & Active States)**: Iridescent Electric Peacock Cyan (`#00DFB6`)
- **Secondary Accent (Chandrika Eye / Streaks)**: Divine Molten Chandrika Gold (`#FFB800`)
- **Flora & Plumes**: Viridescent Peacock Green (`#10B981`)
- **Borders**: Deep Twilight Slate (`#1B3E52`)
- **Typography**: Quill Radiance Cream (`#EEF9F6`) & Feather Sheen Cyan (`#9BC3B9`)

### 3. Dedicated System Mode (प्रणाली अनुसार)
- Diurnal Circadian Synchronization using `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Seamless transition between Raadha daylight and Krishna night.
- Includes hardware diagnostic sensors and one-click diurnal OS simulation for testing.
